# GOAL

## Final Deliverable

둘레길고고 안전·산/코스 데이터·콘텐츠·공개 정책·자동 발행·SEO 개선 및 검증

## User Value

Visitors must not receive fabricated emergency contacts, unsupported safety thresholds, or trail segments presented as verified whole routes. Operators need repeatable evidence and a safe continuation path without losing existing URLs, tracker records, content, or source data.

## Required Features

- Current baseline and inventories with environment limitations.
- P0 safety and route/GPX exposure containment plus recurrence tests.
- Dry-run-first plan for identity, list membership, route evidence, and content review.
- Final readiness and next-action reports that separate local code, tests, production state, and unknowns.

## Non-Goals

- Production deployment/DB mutation, bulk visibility/noindex changes, AdSense/CMP/account actions, invented human review/source facts, or a redesign.

## Done Conditions

- Confirmed P0 claims are removed from local render paths and blocked at publication.
- Segment metrics are not described as verified full routes or beginner recommendations.
- Tests/typecheck/build and representative smoke proof are recorded, or gaps are explicit.
- Full requested scope is either verified or itemized with file/record/command-level continuation.

## User-Visible Result

Safer guidance and honest data labels after a future authorized release; no claim of production application before deployment proof.
