## Official evidence used

- U.S. National Library of Medicine MedlinePlus headache danger signs: sudden severe headache and neurologic changes, fever/stiff neck, head injury, or other acute warning signs require urgent evaluation.
  <https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000424.htm>
- CDC/NIOSH heat-related illness: altered mental state, slurred speech, seizure, or loss of consciousness are heatstroke warning signs requiring emergency response and rapid cooling.
  <https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/heat-stress/about/illnesses.html>
- CDC Yellow Book heat/cold illness: headache, nausea, weakness, and lethargy overlap between heat exhaustion and exercise-associated hyponatremia; forced water intake is not a field diagnostic test.
  <https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/heat-and-cold-illness-in-travelers.html>
- CDC Yellow Book altitude illness: diagnosis depends on recent high-altitude ascent plus symptoms; do not ascend with symptoms and descend if they worsen at the same elevation.
  <https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/high-altitude-travel-and-altitude-illness.html>
