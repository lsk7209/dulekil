## Replacement contract

- Treat current managing-agency closure and route status as a non-negotiable first filter.
- Evaluate full-route demands, the slowest party member's current functional state, actual local sunset and current weather without converting them to universal numeric approval scores.
- Severe uncertainty or one material stop condition cannot be cancelled out by strengths elsewhere; choose progress, reduced route or cancellation explicitly.
- Treat emergency access and arrival time as uncertain; call 119 early with location and condition instead of attempting risky self-evacuation based on a predicted delay.
