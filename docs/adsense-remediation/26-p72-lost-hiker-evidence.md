## Replacement contract

- Stop uncertain movement, identify current hazards, and record the last confirmed location before choosing any safe movement.
- Call 119 early for injury, exposure illness, darkness, severe weather or inability to find a safe route; share multiple location clues and condition information.
- Treat GPS, apps and alternate reporting as conditional aids rather than prerequisites or guarantees; confirm receipt and preserve the callback path.
- Move only to escape a continuing hazard or under current responder guidance, and report the new location after unavoidable movement.
- Manage battery according to communication needs without fixed capacity or automatic airplane-mode/power-off advice; share the trip plan directly with a person.
