## Official evidence used

- CDC frostbite prevention and first aid: pale/gray-yellow, firm/waxy or numb skin suggests frostbite; seek medical care, check hypothermia, avoid unnecessary walking, rubbing and direct heat, and use warm-not-hot water only in the stated limited setting.
  <https://www.cdc.gov/winter-weather/prevention/preventing-frostbite.html>
- U.S. National Library of Medicine MedlinePlus: treat hypothermia first, protect and separate digits, avoid rewarming if refreezing can occur, avoid direct heat/rubbing/blister disturbance, and seek care.
  <https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000057.htm>
- CDC Yellow Book: search for and treat coexisting hypothermia first and use rapid water rewarming in a controlled safe range when appropriate.
  <https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/heat-and-cold-illness-in-travelers.html>
