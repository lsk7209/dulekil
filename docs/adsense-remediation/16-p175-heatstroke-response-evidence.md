## Official evidence used

- Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency heat-illness response: move to a cool place, cool the body, call 119 for unconsciousness, and do not give drinks to an unconscious person.
  <https://www.kdca.go.kr/contents.es?mid=a20205030103>
- CDC/NIOSH heat-related illnesses: heatstroke can present with hot dry skin or profuse sweating; confusion, altered mental status, slurred speech, seizure, or loss of consciousness require emergency care and rapid cooling, including cold/ice-water methods when available.
  <https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/heat-stress/about/illnesses.html>
- CDC Yellow Book: heat exhaustion and exercise-associated hyponatremia symptoms overlap, so headache/nausea/weakness cannot safely be reduced to one hydration diagnosis.
  <https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/heat-and-cold-illness-in-travelers.html>
